Roof Leak Water Damage in Westland: What to Know

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. Water enters at a raised shingle, a split in the flashing or a worn skylight seal, follows the rafters and decking downhill, and finally lands on the insulation. A ceiling stain tends to arrive late, well after the space overhead first got wet. Our team maps everything that got wet, then dries it.

To be clear, roof repair is not what we do, water damage is. Our job is the inside of the house: pulling out soaked insulation, drying the framing and drywall, treating for mold and putting the ceiling back together. Should the leak still be active, our crew can cover that section with a tarp for now, or time our work with the roofing contractor you pick so nothing gets sealed over an open leak.

Your First Steps

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Catch the DripsFurniture, electronics and rugs should be moved aside, with buckets placed wherever water is falling. Plastic or towels spread on the floor will keep it from soaking.
  3. Drain a Bulging CeilingIf the ceiling is holding a pocket of water, stand to one side and poke a small hole at the lowest point so it drains into a bucket.
  4. Leave the Roof AloneA wet roof and an unlit attic are both places to stay out of. Note from inside where the water is coming in and leave the rest to professionals.
  5. Document What You SeeBefore anything is moved or discarded, get video and photos of the attic, the ceiling and damaged items, and then phone us so drying can begin.
Why It Happens

What Causes It

  • Storm impact, such as hail or a tree branch, breaking through the shingles.
  • Shingles that are cracked, curled or blown off by wind, exposing the underlayment.
  • Gutters packed with leaves, which push water back beneath the first row of shingles.
  • A vent pipe boot whose rubber has split from long sun exposure.
  • Fasteners working loose over time, so water follows the nail shank into the attic.
What Is at Risk

What the Water Damages

Attic Insulation

Wet insulation mats down, stops insulating and holds water against the ceiling drywall for days. In some cases fiberglass batts dry out and stay. Removal is the usual answer for wet cellulose.

Wall Cavities

Water that reaches the top of a wall runs down inside it, wetting insulation and the back of the drywall. You might notice nothing more than a peeling patch or a puffy baseboard.

Rafters and Decking

Rafters, trusses and decking that get wet again and again begin to darken, swell and soften. Decay here means less strength in what carries the roof.

Lights and Wiring

Because fixtures are holes in the drywall, a leak often shows up at a light or fan first. Wet connections can short or corrode, so the circuit should stay off until it is checked.

Coverage

Insurance and Roof Leak Water Damage

When a leak happens suddenly and by accident, for example a branch going through the roof or wind stripping shingles, many homeowners policies pay for the water damage inside. Insurers generally turn down claims tied to wear, age or lack of upkeep, and they may handle the roof repair on different terms than the inside damage. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.

Cost

What Affects the Cost

  • The length of time the leak went unnoticed, plus whether mold has started.
  • How much repainting is required so the patched ceiling blends in.
  • The amount of ceiling and wall drywall that needs to be cut out.
  • Tight attics, tall ceilings and the need for an emergency tarp at the start.

Our Process

Our Roof Leak Water Damage Process

1

Trace the Leak Path

Using thermal imaging and moisture meters, our team checks wall cavities, ceilings and the attic and charts the whole route the water took, even where the room below shows nothing.

2

Tarp and Protect

An exposed roof gets covered with a temporary tarp, or we line things up with your roofing contractor. Floors and belongings below the wet ceiling are covered as well.

3

Remove and Dry

Unsalvageable drywall and insulation come out, and dehumidifiers and air movers go into the attic space and each room under it, with readings taken until the framing has dried.

4

Sanitize and Repair

Exposed wood gets an antimicrobial treatment where needed. With the roof fixed, our team installs insulation and drywall, finishes the joints, and primes and paints so the repair blends in.
