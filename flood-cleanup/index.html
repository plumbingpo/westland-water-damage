Signs It Is Time for Flood Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A water line on walls or baseboards showing how high it rose
  • Any amount of water sitting in a room, garage or basement
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through
  • Appliances such as a water heater, washer or dishwasher spilling water across the floor

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Being outside a designated flood zone does not rule out flooding, so think about flood coverage
  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
  • Have a backwater valve fitted to the sewer line so sewage cannot flow back inside
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation
  • Add downspout extensions so roof water empties well away from the foundation
  • In areas of the property that tend to flood, raise electrical systems and appliances off the floor
