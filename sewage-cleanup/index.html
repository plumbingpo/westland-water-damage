Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Westland Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Westland, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Roots from trees close to the sewer line work their way into pipes and block them, so have them cleared
  • Food waste, oils and grease block pipes, so put them in the trash and not the sink
  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it

Signs It Is Time for Sewage Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Several fixtures backing up at once instead of only one
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels

Our Sewage Cleanup Process

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle sewage cleanup from start to finish:

1

Safety Assessment

Our team assesses the extent of sewage contamination, identifies safety hazards and establishes containment to prevent cross-contamination of unaffected areas.

2

Extracting Sewage and Removing Materials

We extract every bit of standing sewage. Porous materials that were contaminated, such as drywall, insulation, carpet and padding, come out and go to proper disposal as biohazard waste.

3

We thoroughly clean every surface that remains, then disinfect it with an EPA-registered antimicrobial solution. Meanwhile, air scrubbers fitted with HEPA filters pull pathogens and other contaminants from the air.

4

Dry-Out and Deodorizing

Residual moisture is taken out with commercial-grade dryers. An industrial deodorizing treatment gets rid of the sewage smell. Clearance testing confirms the space is safe for occupancy.

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.
