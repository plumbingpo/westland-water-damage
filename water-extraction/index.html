Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. Across Westland, Westland Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early
  • Put together a simple kit with a bucket, some old towels and the phone number of your restoration company
  • Critical areas of the house should each have a leak sensor that you know is working
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply
  • Check the supply hoses on your appliances and swap out any that look old or worn

How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • Visible water where your furnace, water heater or crawl space access is
  • Seams in the floor that ooze water under foot pressure
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • An appliance, HVAC unit or pipe that is leaking right now
  • Moisture appearing along the bottom of walls or at cabinet toe kicks
