Mold is more than an unsightly nuisance: it is a serious health hazard and a sign of underlying moisture problems that threaten the structure of your property. Mold exposure can cause respiratory issues, allergic reactions, headaches and more severe health effects, particularly in children, the elderly and people with compromised immune systems. At Westland Restoration, our IICRC-certified mold remediation technicians follow strict industry protocols to safely contain, remove and prevent mold in Westland homes and commercial properties.

Real remediation involves a great deal more than cleaning visible growth from a wall. Done professionally, the work includes finding where the moisture comes from, containing the area so spores cannot cross-contaminate other rooms, taking out affected material under HEPA filtration, applying antimicrobials to what remains and correcting the moisture issue to prevent a return. Working this way removes the mold where it starts instead of covering it up temporarily.

We begin every mold remediation project with a thorough inspection and assessment, often including air quality testing to determine the type and concentration of mold spores present. This scientific approach lets us develop a targeted remediation plan for your specific situation. While the work is underway, the area stays under negative air pressure, HEPA scrubbers clean the air and our crew follows IICRC S520, the mold remediation standard. Afterward we verify the results, with clearance air testing if you request it, to confirm the property is safe to occupy.

The Benefits of Professional Mold Remediation

Removing mold is work homeowners should not attempt on their own. Without containment, scrubbing or tearing out moldy material releases spores by the millions, potentially spreading contamination through the house and making the health exposure worse than it was. Our crews work in personal protective equipment inside a negative-pressure enclosure, with HEPA air scrubbers running, to trap spores and hold them in the work area while removal is underway.

A professional job goes after the moisture source too, because that is why the mold grew in the first place. Removing visible mold without correcting the underlying moisture problem means the mold will return, often within weeks. Certified remediation technicians have the training and equipment to identify hidden moisture sources, dry affected areas properly and put solutions in place that prevent recurrence.

How to Tell You Need Mold Remediation

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Fuzzy or spotted growth on stored boxes, clothing or the backs of furniture
  • Feeling better away from home, then having coughs, headaches or allergies come back indoors
  • Peeling wallpaper, bubbling paint or warped materials that point to hidden moisture
  • Windows, cold surfaces or walls that sweat with condensation on a regular basis
  • Long-running hidden leaks, for example at a dishwasher line, sink drain or roof flashing
  • Spots you wipe away that keep reappearing in the same place
  • Recent water damage or flooding that was not professionally dried

How to Prevent Mold Remediation Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Clear gutters and downspouts regularly, which keeps rain from soaking the foundation walls
  • Use ventilation and a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60%, with 30 to 50% as the ideal
  • Mold in ductwork is prevented by servicing the heating and cooling system and replacing its filters on schedule
  • Deal quickly with condensation that forms on pipes, cold surfaces or window glass
  • Give the crawl space proper ventilation, and think about adding a vapor barrier over the soil
  • Mold-resistant paint and wallboard are the better choice wherever moisture is common, such as a basement or bath
